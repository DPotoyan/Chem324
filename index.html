Quantum Mechanics for Chemistry

Welcome to Chem 3240: Quantum Mechanics for Chemistry, Fall 2026.

These notes follow the historical path into quantum theory: from the puzzles of classical physics through the birth of quantization, wave-particle duality, and the Schrödinger equation, building toward the quantum description of atoms and molecules.

Fall 2026 schedule¶

Homework is due the following Monday at the start of class (due dates are given with each assignment). No homework is due in exam week 12 or over Thanksgiving.
